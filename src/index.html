A 4-player co-op retro dungeon crawler built for short, intense delves. Classic holy trinity gameplay — roles, not locked classes. Bring any composition, but you need the trinity to survive.

Core Loop

4-Player Co-op

Drop-in / drop-out. Trinity scales naturally — 1 Tank, 1 Healer, 2 DPS flex slots.

Holy Trinity

Tank controls aggro and mitigation. Healer manages mana and positioning. DPS executes mechanics.

Short Delves

8–12 minute missions. Procedural-lite hand-authored chunks. Perfect for a lunch break run.

Retro Crawler

First-person grid dungeon with CRT phosphor aesthetic. Chunky pixels, arcade speed, deliberate feel.
